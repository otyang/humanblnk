Customers and accounts

Scenario: Ada signs up. She gets a naira wallet straight away, and later a dollar wallet. Your company also needs an escrow pool of its own.

Step 1: the owner

Every customer account has an owner: a Blnk identity that holds no personal data. Ada's name, phone number and KYC stay in your own customer database; the identity holds only references such as your customer id.

Find it, or make it the first time, so a retried sign-up doesn't make a second one. Identities.Create on its own isn't safe to repeat.

Step 2: open her wallets

OpenForCustomer is safe to repeat. A customer has at most one account per ledger and currency, and calling it again returns that one. So you can call it whenever you need Ada's wallet, instead of storing ids everywhere.

CustomerAccount field
Ownerrequired
Currencyone per account: a second currency is a second account
Ledgeroptional: empty is the WithAccountLedger one. Use your Savings ledger for a savings account. Must be a customer ledger.

Step 3: read balances

An Account has three amounts:

FieldMeaning
Totaleverything recorded
Heldreserved by holds not yet captured or cancelled
AvailableTotal less held and queued debits: what she can spend now

Always decide "can she pay?" on Available, read with Get. List is quicker for many accounts, but its Available leaves queued debits out.

Step 4: tag the owner later

UpdateMeta merges: keys you don't send keep their values. References only, never personal data.

Your company's own accounts

An account that belongs to your company uses CreateInternal, in a ledger that isn't a customer one. The owner is optional. Unlike OpenForCustomer, it makes a new account on every call: if it times out, look with List first.

The rules hold both ways, so mistakes fail early:

CallResult
OpenForCustomer without an ownerErrInvalid
OpenForCustomer in a ledger that isn't a customer ledgerErrInvalid
CreateInternal in a customer ledgerErrInvalid: use OpenForCustomer
CreateInternal in the General LedgerErrInvalid: Blnk makes system accounts itself

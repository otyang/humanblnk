Chapter 5 · Moving money

Transfers and fees

Scenario: Ada sends Bola ₦10,000. You charge a 1.5% fee on top. The app shows Ada what will happen before she confirms, and refuses if she can't afford it.

Step 1: build the transfer as one split

The payment to Bola and your fee are one transaction with two postings on the To side. It applies in full or not at all: Bola is never paid without the fee being recorded, or the other way round.

Both sides must add up to the same amount, or it's ErrUnbalanced. One side may have several postings, but not both: Blnk moves one account to many, or many to one.

Step 2: the dry run

Preview checks the transaction exactly as Record would, funds included, but moves nothing and leaves the reference unused:

When it wouldn't apply, the reason is in p.Rejection and err is nil. errors.Is(p.Rejection, humanblnk.ErrInsufficientFunds) works.

A preview is not a reservation. Another payment can spend the money between Preview and Record, and Record checks funds again. To reserve money, use a hold (chapter 6).

Step 4: her history

Newest first. List shows Blnk's own records, each posting of a split as its own entry. Record sends a split as a hold and captures it at once (see below), so Blnk keeps each posting twice: held (StatusHeld) and captured (StatusApplied, under the transfer's reference). The example asks for StatusApplied: the money that moved. Blnk can't combine "from or to" with other conditions, so a full history is two queries: one with From, one with To. EntryQuery also filters by Status, Since and Until.

The whole example

go run ./docs/examples/05-transfers

Good to know

  • A split that fails partway moves nothing. Blnk moves a split one posting at a time: applied directly, a later posting that fails leaves the earlier ones moved until Blnk's worker refunds them. So Record holds the split first and captures it at once. A hold that fails partway is released before Blnk answers: Record returns the error and no account ever shows the earlier postings. The entry still reads as one applied transaction, with the id Record returned.
  • Retrying that split with the same Ref returns ErrReferenceUsed, not ErrDuplicate: nothing stands under that reference, so record it again under a new one (chapter 12).

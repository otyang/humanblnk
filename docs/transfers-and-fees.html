Chapter 5 · Moving money

Transfers and fees

Scenario: Ada sends Bola ₦10,000. HumanBlnk charges a 1.5% fee on top. The app shows Ada what will happen before she confirms, and refuses if she can't afford it.

Step 1: build the transfer as one split

The payment to Bola and HumanBlnk's fee are one transaction with two postings on the To side. It applies in full or not at all: Bola is never paid without the fee being recorded, or the other way round.

Both sides must add up to the same amount, or it's ErrUnbalanced. One side may have several postings, but not both: Blnk moves one account to many, or many to one.

Step 2: the dry run

Preview checks the transaction exactly as Record would, funds included, but moves nothing and leaves the reference unused:

When it wouldn't apply, the reason is in p.Rejection and err is nil. errors.Is(p.Rejection, ledger.ErrInsufficientFunds) works.

A preview is not a reservation. Another payment can spend the money between Preview and Record, and Record checks funds again. To reserve money, use a hold (chapter 6).

Step 4: her history

Newest first. List shows each posting of a split as its own entry (…-1, …-2). Blnk can't combine "from or to" with other conditions, so a full history is two queries: one with From, one with To. EntryQuery also filters by Status, Since and Until.

The whole example

go run ./docs/examples/05-transfers

Good to know

  • A split that fails partway. Blnk records a split one posting at a time. If a later posting fails, the earlier ones have already moved, and Blnk refunds them from its background worker a moment later (about 200 ms). Until then they show in the accounts. Record still returns the error. Keep Blnk's worker running and monitored.
  • Retrying that split with the same Ref returns ErrReferenceUsed, not ErrDuplicate: nothing stands under that reference, so record it again under a new one (chapter 12).

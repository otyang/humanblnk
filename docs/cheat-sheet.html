What comes back

PreviewWouldApply, Rejection, Changes []AccountChange, Notes
IdentityID, Meta, CreatedAt
EventKind, Entry, Watch, Account, Data

Entry statuses: StatusApplied (money moved), StatusHeld (a hold), StatusVoid (a released hold), StatusRejected (refused or rolled back), StatusQueued.

Ids

HoldID, WatchID, StatementID, RuleID and RunID are strings; convert an id you stored, as in humanblnk.HoldID(s). Owner (idt_…) and ledger (ldg_…) ids are plain strings.

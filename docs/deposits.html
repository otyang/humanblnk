Chapter 4 · Moving money

Deposits

Scenario: Ada pays ₦25,000 into her wallet by bank transfer through Paystack. Paystack's webhook tells HumanBlnk, then sends it again. Days later Paystack settles the money into HumanBlnk's bank account.

Where the money comes from

When the webhook arrives, Paystack has the money, not HumanBlnk. So the deposit comes from Paystack's clearing account, humanblnk.ClearingAccount("paystack") (@PaystackClearing). It goes below zero: Paystack owes HumanBlnk that much. When Paystack settles, it returns to zero, so a balance left in a clearing account is money still in transit.

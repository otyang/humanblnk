Chapter 10 · Running it

Watches and webhooks

Scenario: HumanBlnk texts Ada when her balance drops below ₦2,000. Blnk tells HumanBlnk through a webhook, and HumanBlnk must be sure each webhook really came from Blnk.

Step 1: watch the balance

A watch is a threshold on one account. While it holds, Blnk sends the webhook event balance.monitor after every update of the account.

FieldValues
FieldWatchBalance (Total), WatchCredits, WatchDebits, WatchHeld (held out of it), WatchIncomingHeld, WatchHeldNet
OpWatchAbove, WatchAtLeast, WatchBelow, WatchAtMost, WatchEquals, WatchNotEquals
Thresholda money.Money in the account's currency, at most 2^53 in its smallest unit (about ₦90 trillion, or 0.009 ETH). Larger is ErrInvalid: Blnk can't store it exactly.

Step 2: move the line

Change returns a watch with a new id: Blnk can't change a threshold in place, so the new watch is made before the old one is deleted. The account is never unwatched in between, but both may fire once. List with "" lists every watch.

Step 3: receive webhooks safely

Blnk signs each webhook with its server.secret_key. humanblnk.VerifyWebhook checks the signature and the age, then decodes it. Never act on a body that fails the check. This is the handler you mount at the URL Blnk sends webhooks to:

Event field
Kindthe event, such as "balance.monitor" or "transaction.applied"
Watch, Accountfor balance.monitor: which watch fired, on which account
Entryfor transaction.* events: the entry
Datathe raw data, as Blnk sent it

The tolerance rejects old deliveries replayed by an attacker; five minutes is sensible. The example signs requests the way Blnk does and sends a genuine one and a forged one to a test server.

Step 4: stop watching

The whole example

go run ./docs/examples/10-watches-and-webhooks

To receive real webhooks, set Blnk's webhook URL to your handler, and give your service the same secret as Blnk's server.secret_key (see Blnk's configuration docs).

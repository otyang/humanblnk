Chapter 6 · Moving money

Payouts and holds

Scenario: Ada withdraws ₦20,000 to her bank. The money must leave her spendable balance at once, but only really leaves when the bank confirms. A second withdrawal is rejected by the bank. Then she takes a ride: ₦6,000 is reserved on the estimate, and ₦4,200 is charged.

How a hold works

  1. Place reserves the amount. It leaves From's Available at once, but doesn't reach To yet.
  2. When the outside world answers, Capture moves it (all or part; the rest is released), or Cancel gives it all back.
  3. An optional expiry cancels the hold if neither happens in time.

Step 1: place the hold

While it's held, Accounts.Get shows Held 20,000 and Available 20,000 less. Store the hold id with your payout, so the bank's callback can find it. Placing the same transaction again (a retry) returns the same hold.

Expiry: time.Time{} means none. If a payout's hold expires while the bank actually sent the money, HumanBlnk has paid out without taking it. For payouts, prefer no expiry and a job that chases stuck ones.

A partial capture

The ride reserved ₦6,000 on the estimate; the fare was ₦4,200. ₦4,200 moves and the other ₦1,800 goes back to Ada at once:

A capture is final: whatever isn't captured is released. A split hold (a payout and its fee, say) can only be captured in full.

Retrying a partial capture after a timeout: wait a few seconds first. Blnk can't tell a repeated partial capture from a new one, so a retry that arrives while Blnk is still processing the first can capture twice. Full captures and cancels don't have this problem.

The whole example

Good to know

  • A hold's own transaction stays StatusHeld (INFLIGHT) for good. The capture and the release are separate transactions under it, so don't use the hold's status to ask "did it settle?". Capture's return value, or ErrHoldSettled, tells you.
  • To undo money a capture already moved, use Transactions.Reverse (chapter 8). Cancel only releases money still held.

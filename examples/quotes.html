Quotes for confirm screens

Show the customer the fee, the total and their balance after, and whether the payment will go through, before anything moves.

The situation

Before Ada taps Confirm, the Oja app shows: "Send ₦12,500 to Tunde. Fee ₦10. Total ₦12,510. Balance after ₦47,490." The quote calls give you every figure on that screen. They run the same checks as the real call, then ask Blnk to dry-run it, so Allowed is Blnk's own answer. Nothing is posted.

BeforeQuote withThen call
A top-upwallet.QuoteDepositwallet.Deposit
A bank withdrawalwallet.QuoteWithdrawwallet.Withdraw
A payment to another accountwallet.QuoteTransferwallet.Transfer
A currency conversionwallet.QuoteExchangewallet.Exchange

Each quote takes the same input struct as the call it previews.

Quote, then execute

  • Build the input once, with its Reference, when the confirm screen opens. Quote it, and send the same input when the customer confirms.
  • Config.Fees prices the quote exactly as it will price the call. It runs again on the real call; if prices can change in between, set Fee: q.Fee on the call to charge what you showed.
  • q.String() gives a one-line summary for logs.

The fields of a Quote

FieldMeaning
Amountthe amount as entered: for a deposit the gross received, otherwise what the recipient gets
Feethe fee, from Fee or Config.Fees
Debitthe total leaving the paying side: amount plus fee for a transfer or withdrawal; the gross for a deposit
Creditwhat reaches the recipient: the net amount for a deposit, Amount otherwise
Accountthe customer account the quote is about: the sender, or the account topped up
AvailableBeforethat account's available balance now
AvailableAfterits available balance after the call
Allowedwhether the call would go through
Reasonwhy not, when Allowed is false; test it with errors.Is
CheckedByLedgertrue when Blnk dry-ran it; false when a local check already refused it

Deposits and withdrawals

For a deposit, Debit is the gross amount and Credit the amount Ada will get, so AvailableAfter goes up by the net.

When the answer is no

A business-rule refusal is not an error. The quote comes back with Allowed: false and the reason, so the app can explain it.

These five errors come back as Reason:

ReasonShow the customer
ErrInsufficientFunds"Your balance is too low"; AvailableAfter shows by how much
ErrLimitExceeded"This is over your limit"; wallet.LimitUsage says what is left
ErrAccountFrozen"This account can't receive or send money right now"
ErrAccountClosed"This account is closed"
ErrLedgerFrozen"Payments are paused"

A quote that is refused locally, like the frozen recipient here, never reaches Blnk, so CheckedByLedger is false.

Errors

Malformed input is an error, as it would be on the real call.

ErrorWhenWhat to do
ErrInvalidInputno Reference; too many decimal places; a bad rate; no fee and no Config.Fees; the same account on both sidesfix the input
ErrWrongKinda house account where a customer account is requiredpass a customer account
ErrDuplicateReferencethe reference is already used, e.g. quoting again after the callquote before the call; give a new payment a new reference
ErrFeePricingConfig.Fees failedfix the pricing function
ErrUnavailableBlnk can't be reachedshow a retry message
